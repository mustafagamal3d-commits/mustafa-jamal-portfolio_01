Normal Map Generator & Texture Suite

Generate tangent-space normal maps from height or color textures with real-time 3D PBR lighting inspection.

Pro Tip & Disclaimer: Normal maps generated from 2D pixel luminance are mathematical gradient approximations. For hero cinematic assets, high-to-low poly cage baking or Substance Painter is recommended.

DirectX vs OpenGL Formats

• OpenGL (+Y): Blender, Maya, Three.js, WebGL, Godot.
• DirectX (-Y): Unreal Engine 4/5, 3ds Max, Unity (default).
Use the "Invert Green" toggle to match your target game engine or DCC tool.

Sobel vs Scharr Kernels

• Sobel: Standard 3x3 convolution with smooth gradient transitions. Ideal for organic fabrics and leather.
• Scharr: Optimized rotational symmetry for sharp geometric edges, masonry, and mechanical hard-surface relief.
