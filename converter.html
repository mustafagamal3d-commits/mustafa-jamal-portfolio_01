The unpacker will extract the internal JSON scene descriptor, geometry buffers, and uncompressed texture maps into a standard glTF bundle.

Why Package to GLB?

GLB bundles all meshes, animations, and textures into a single binary file. It is the standardized format for Shopify 3D, Apple QuickLook AR, Android Scene Viewer, and Facebook/Instagram 3D posts.

Privacy & Zero Data Leaks

Unlike online converters that store your models on cloud servers, this tool executes Three.js GLTFExporter directly in Web Workers inside your RAM. Your files never touch a server.

Support for PBR Textures

Preserves Base Color, Roughness, Metallic, Normal Maps, Emissive, and Ambient Occlusion channels correctly without loss of color space.
